Book Week Parade and Book Fair

Hi families,

Our Book Week parade will be held on Friday 30 October at 9:15am in the school hall. Students are invited to dress up as their favourite book character. Parents and carers are very welcome to attend.

The Scholastic Book Fair will run in the library from Monday 26 October to Thursday 29 October, open 8:30am to 9:00am and 3:15pm to 3:45pm each day.

Please also note that Monday 2 November is a Student Free Day for staff professional learning. Camp Australia will run OSHC on that day; bookings close Wednesday 28 October.
